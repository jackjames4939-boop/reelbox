How it works FAQ

Paste a link.Get the video.

Save an Instagram video or Reel to your phone or computer as an MP4 file.

Only download videos you own or have permission to use. Private accounts can't be downloaded.

How it works

  1. Copy the link

    In Instagram, open the video, tap Share, then tap Copy link.

  2. Paste it above

    Put the link in the box at the top of this page.

  3. Save the video

    Tap Download video. The MP4 saves to your device.

Questions

Which links work?

Links to public Instagram Reels and video posts, such as instagram.com/reel/… or instagram.com/p/…

My download failed. What can I do?

Check that the account is public and the link is complete. If it still fails, wait a minute and try again. Instagram sometimes blocks requests for a short time.

Can I download from a private account?

No. Only public videos can be downloaded.

Is it okay to reuse a video I downloaded?

Videos belong to their creators. Download and reuse only videos you made or have permission to use.
